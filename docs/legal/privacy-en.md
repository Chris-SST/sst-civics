## 7. How long we keep data

7.1 Account and progress data are kept while your account exists. Sessions last 14 days (rolling). Sign-in links expire after 15 minutes (confirmation links after 24 hours, password reset links after 30 minutes) and are purged a day after expiry.

7.2 You can delete your account yourself on the Account page. You confirm with your password or a fresh sign-in link. We then delete your account, sessions, progress, conversations, reports and access records, and email you a confirmation.

7.3 We keep after deletion:

- **Payment records** (product, canton, amount, currency, dates, Stripe references) for ten years, as Swiss bookkeeping law requires (Code of Obligations, art. 958f). Your account link is removed; we keep only a keyed hash of your email so that we can find a payment if you ask about it.
- **AI cost records** (tokens, cost, model, time), without any identifier or content.
- **Payment event records** from Stripe, reduced to an event reference and type once they name you.
- **Admin audit entries**, without your identity, and one entry recording that your account was deleted.
- **Stripe's own records**, which Stripe keeps under its own legal duties. You may ask us to have your Stripe customer deleted.
- **Backups**, which expire within 30 days. Deletion is complete when the last backup holding your data has expired.

## 8. Security

8.1 Pages and data travel encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as strong one-way hashes. Sign-in links and session tokens are stored only as hashes; links work once and expire quickly.

8.2 Administrator accounts need two-factor authentication, and its secrets are stored encrypted. Access is limited to what is needed. Nightly backups are kept.

8.3 We limit sign-in attempts and link requests to resist abuse. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach is likely to harm you we will notify you and the authority as the law requires.
