# Sources for the Zurich topic list

All pages opened on 30 October 2026.

## Pages used

- https://www.zh.ch/de/migration-integration/einbuergerung/grundkenntnistest.html : test purpose (knowledge of Switzerland, the canton and the Zurich communal system; geography, politics, history), page sections, and the links to the practice test, brochure and full question list. States no question counts, format, duration or pass mark.
- https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/migration-integration/einbuergerung/gkt/grundkenntnistest_kanton_zuerich.pdf : read for the table of contents only. Structure: 1 Demokratie und Föderalismus (Bund, Kanton, Gemeinde); 2 Sozialstaat und Zivilgesellschaft (Bund, Kanton, Gemeinde); 3 Geschichte (Bund, Kanton); 4 Geografie (Bund, Kanton). Our topic groups follow these four sections and three levels.
- https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/migration-integration/einbuergerung/gkt/broschuere_einbuergerung_grundkenntnistest.pdf : could not be read (over the 10 MB fetch limit); not used.
- https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/migration-integration/einbuergerung/publlikationen/Kommunale%20Einb%C3%BCrgerungsprozesse%202025.pdf : listed as a reference for communal naturalisation; only found in search results, not read in full.
- https://www.zh.ch/de/politik-staat/gesetze-beschluesse.html and https://www.zh.ch/de/politik-staat/gesetze-beschluesse/gesetzessammlung/zhlex-ls/erlass-101-2005_02_27-2006_01_01-129.html : the Zurich cantonal constitution (LS 101, 27 February 2005, in force 1 January 2006). The link was taken from the legal collection page; its text and headings were not read, so the cantonal topics rest on the search summary (Grundlagen, Grundrechte, citizenship, popular rights) plus general knowledge of the structure.
- https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1999/404/de : Federal Constitution (SR 101). The page needs JavaScript and returned no headings; the federal backbone (fundamental rights, Confederation/cantons/communes, people and cantons, federal authorities, revision) is from general knowledge, not verified on this page.
- https://www.bk.admin.ch/bund-kurz-erklaert : listed on the Zurich page as preparation material; used as a reference for federal topic names, not read.
- https://www.ch.ch/de/abstimmungen-und-wahlen/initiativen/was-ist-eine-eidgenossische-volksinitiative/ and https://www.ch.ch/de/abstimmungen-und-wahlen/referendum/ : popular initiative and referendum topics (via search results).
- https://www.ch.ch/de/ and https://www.ch.ch/de/einbuergerung : general topic names; the ch.ch "staat-und-politik" URL returned a 404, so German and English names are my own.
- A Maur communal flyer (https://www.maur.ch/public/upload/assets/4902/Flyer%20Grundkenntnistest.pdf?fp=3), seen only in search results, mentions a 350-question catalogue from which 50 are asked. This is not an official zh.ch page and is not listed in the YAML sources.

## Not confirmed

1. Pass mark of 30 out of 50: from a 2022 press report supplied in the plan; not found on any official page.
2. Test duration of 60 minutes: not stated on the zh.ch page.
3. Per-section question counts: the table of contents gives sections and levels only. The 274/56/20 split comes from the plan; the target counts per topic are my own allocation, summing to 275 / 55 / 20.
4. Also unverified: the full headings of the Federal and Zurich constitutions, and any catalogue sections after page 65 of the question PDF (the table of contents was cut off).

## No catalogue text copied

Only the section and subsection headings of the question catalogue were read, and the topic names in zh.yaml are written in my own words. No question, answer option or answer text from the catalogue, the brochure or any test series was read, copied or paraphrased, in line with rule 8 of the plan.
