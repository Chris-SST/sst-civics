---
lang: en
question: "Where can I find my commune's requirements?"
order: 10
---

On the website of your commune of residence, usually under "Einbürgerung" (naturalisation). There you find the checklist, the fee and dates. In the canton of Zurich the Gemeindeamt also helps, telephone +41 43 259 83 81. See also [Zurich, step by step](/en/zurich-step-by-step/).
