Write to us at **[info@viapunt.ch](mailto:info@viapunt.ch)**. We usually answer within two working days.

## What we can help with

- your account, signing in and your Civics Pass;
- payments and refunds (please write from the email address of your account);
- a question or explanation you think is wrong: you can also use "Report a problem" under the question in the app;
- privacy requests, such as a copy of your data.

## What we cannot help with

We give no legal advice and cannot assess your own application. For questions about your application, contact your commune or the [Gemeindeamt of the canton of Zurich](https://www.zh.ch/de/migration-integration/einbuergerung.html).
