# Content notes

**Machine drafts, 30 October and 3 November 2026.** The French, Italian, Portuguese, Spanish, Albanian
and Turkish pages in `guide/{fr,it,pt,es,sq,tr}/` and `faq/{fr,it,pt,es,sq,tr}/` are machine
translations from the English source (German as reference for institutional terms). They await
native-speaker review and must not be treated as final. Facts, figures, source lists and dates are
copied unchanged from the English pages. Since 3 November 2026 every English guide page exists in all
eight languages (15 pages each): the overview, Zurich step by step, documents, the knowledge test, the
naturalisation interview, six pages on the areas of the Zurich catalogue (democracy and federalism,
social state, history, geography, culture, canton and communes), language certificates, fees,
calculator and free practice. Translated pages link to pages in their own language.

The German pages of 3 November 2026 (knowledge test rewritten, interview and the six catalogue-area
pages new) were also drafted by machine from the English and need a read by a German-speaking reviewer.
